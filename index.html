Cozy Merge Town – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026 · App version 1.0.0

In short: Cozy Merge Town does not collect your personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it does not send any data to us or to anyone else. Your game progress stays on your device.

1. Who we are

Cozy Merge Town (the “app”) is published by Serdica Studio (“we”, “us”). If you have a question about this policy or your data, contact us at contact@serdicastudio.com.

2. What we collect

We do not collect, receive or store any information about you. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, age, location or any other personal information, and it has no sign-in.

3. What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The Android release does not request the internet permission and contains no code that sends data to a server.
  • It does not contain advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • It does not contain analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • It does not offer in-app purchases or take any payment.
  • It does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or location, and it requests no permissions that you need to grant.

4. Data stored on your device

To let you continue playing, the app saves the following on your device only, in the app’s private storage:

  • your game progress: the board, coins, level and experience, customer orders, renovations of your shops, the collection of items you have found, stored items, energy and lives, and the stars and progress you earned on the level maps;
  • small settings and flags, for example which tutorial hints you have already seen and your table style choice;
  • a random number created when you start playing, used only to choose the game’s customer orders. It is not linked to you or to your device;
  • a short log of your level attempts (level number, taps used, result, stars, duration, and, if you tap one, your “Too easy / Fair / Too hard” rating). It holds only gameplay numbers: no name, device information, location or free text. It keeps at most the latest 300 attempts.

The app uses your device’s clock to work out how much energy and how many lives have recovered. None of this data leaves your device through the app.

5. The gameplay report

On the Levels screen a small Tools panel can copy the attempt log above, as a text report, to your device’s clipboard. This happens only when you tap the button. The app does not send the text anywhere; if you paste it into a message or an email, you decide who receives it. The same panel can clear the log.

6. Device backup

Android may include an app’s local data in your device’s own backup to your Google account if you have device backup turned on. That backup is run by Android and Google under your device settings, not by us, and we have no access to it. See Google’s documentation for how to turn it off.

7. Third-party components

The app is built with the Flutter framework, the Flame game engine and the shared_preferences storage plugin. They run inside the app on your device. They are used for drawing the game and saving it locally, and the app does not use them to send data anywhere.

8. Deleting your data

  • In the app, open Settings → Reset Progress to erase your game progress and start over.
  • Use the Clear button in the Tools panel to erase the attempt log.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the app’s local data from your device. Depending on your settings, Android backup (if it is turned on) may keep a copy of that data in your Google account (see section 6). We cannot see or delete that copy; you can manage or delete it in your device and Google account backup settings.

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.

9. Children

The app does not ask for or knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. It has no chat, no accounts, no ads and no links that leave the app.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example if a future version adds purchases or online features, we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.
